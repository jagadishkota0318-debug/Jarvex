Frequently Asked Questions
What's included in each package?
Every package includes domain registration (1 year), hosting (1 year) with SSL, mobile responsive design, SEO setup, blog setup, WhatsApp integration, contact forms, and GA4/Google Tag Manager setup. No hidden costs.
What happens after the first year?
Domain and hosting renewals are billed annually at standard rates. Your website, content, and all assets are fully yours — we hand over all credentials and source code.
Can I add more pages or products later?
Absolutely. Extra pages and additional product uploads can be added at any time. Reach out and we'll share the details based on your package.
How quickly can you deliver?
Starter websites are delivered in 7–10 days, Growth packages in 10–15 days, and E-Commerce stores in 15–25 days. Timelines start after the discovery call and content handoff.
Do you also offer Google & Meta Ads, App Development, or AI services?
Yes! We offer performance marketing (Google & Meta Ads), mobile app development, and AI integration services separately. Contact us for a custom quote tailored to your needs.
