Frequently Asked Questions
Can I cancel or change my plan at any time?
Yes — all plans are month-to-month with no long-term contracts. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel with 30 days notice. No penalties, no questions asked.
What happens after the monthly retainer ends?
Any assets created (website, app, ad creatives) are fully yours. We hand over all credentials, source code, and documentation at the end of the engagement.
Do you offer one-time project pricing?
Yes. If you need a single deliverable — a website, an app, or an ad campaign setup — we offer fixed-price project quotes. Contact us for a custom estimate.
How quickly can you start?
Most projects kick off within 5–7 business days of signing. We always do a discovery call first to make sure we're fully aligned before any work begins.
Is there a free trial or pilot option?
We offer a free 30-min strategy call for all new clients. For large-scale engagements, we can structure a paid pilot sprint so you see results before committing fully.
